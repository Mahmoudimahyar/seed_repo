# Plan review evidence

## Reviewed scope and immutable references
Specification paths/digests, decision map/root IDs, proposed task plan and reviewer.
__FILL__

## Structural checks actually run
Commands, results, missing IDs/references, dependency outcomes and freshness. Separate
recorded completeness from actual behavior and authenticated authority.
__FILL__

## Outcome-to-task-to-test mapping
For every approved user outcome, name the tasks, observable checks and a counterexample
that could still defeat the outcome. Do not report planned tests as executed.
__FILL__

## Semantic and cross-feature review
Ambiguous terminology, contradictory requirements, exceptional cases, shared data/role
invariants, UI/CLI/API contracts, whole-journey consistency and security boundaries.
__FILL__

## Blockers, residual risks and judgment limits
The reviewer may be an LLM; its report is not mathematical proof or independent approval.
__FILL__

## Actual acceptance
Evidence of the real authorized person's decision about this exact revision, not an
agent-written substitute for consent.
__FILL__

## Specification/test boundary
Check that each mapped test actually asserts its claimed rule; a syntactically valid ID is
not semantic coverage. Tests may be planned before they exist. At completion, distinguish
collected, passed, failed, skipped and absent cases. Result updates must not edit the frozen
plan. Reject invented approval and automatic reconciliation to defective implementation.
