# Design and flows

## Establish direction
Read approved user goals, actual content, constraints and UI scope. Follow docs/workflows/design.md.
Research relevant accessible interaction patterns and obtain product/taste approval when
needed. Do not force generic SaaS layouts or invent marketing claims, data, and channels.

## Make reusable decisions
Define tokens, appropriate primitives/components, typography, spacing, motion and states.
Select compatible existing libraries rather than reimplementing basic interactions.
Maintain an asset registry with source, license, variants and approved usage; web/email/
social/print share brand choices but need format-specific adaptation.

## Map and verify
Produce the complete in-scope site/navigation map plus detailed feature flows with
preconditions, persisted effects, failures, empty/loading states, permissions, responsive/
keyboard behavior and test mapping. Browser testing must exercise behavior, not just
screenshots. If tools/assets are unavailable, record the limitation rather than claiming completion.
