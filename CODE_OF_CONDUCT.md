# Community conduct

Be respectful, specific, and constructive. Critique ideas and code without attacking
people. Welcome contributors with different backgrounds and experience levels. Do not
harass, threaten, discriminate, disclose personal information, impersonate others, or
publish confidential material. Do not flood issues or PRs with automated, unreviewed work.

Maintainers may edit/remove harmful content, close disruptive discussions, or restrict
participation. Decisions should be proportionate, documented where safe, and handled by
someone without a direct conflict when possible. Good-faith technical disagreement is welcome.

Use GitHub's **Report content** or other private platform reporting tools for harassment
or abuse; do not amplify private details in a public thread. Maintainers should publish
additional private moderation contact options when established. Security vulnerabilities
belong in the process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not ordinary discussion.

This original, concise policy applies to the repository's issues, PRs, discussions, and
other explicitly designated community spaces. It does not claim adoption of a separately
licensed external code-of-conduct template.
